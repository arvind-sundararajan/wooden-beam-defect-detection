# Knowledge Graph Design
## Introduction
The Knowledge Graph is a graph database that stores information about the wooden beams, including their properties, defects, and optimization suggestions. The Knowledge Graph is designed to be scalable, flexible, and queryable, allowing for easy integration with different components of the system.
## Knowledge Graph Components
The Knowledge Graph consists of the following components:
* **Beam Nodes**: represent the wooden beams, including their properties and defects.
* **Defect Nodes**: represent the defects detected in the wooden beams, including their type, location, and severity.
* **Optimization Nodes**: represent the optimization suggestions, including repairs, replacements, and maintenance.
* **Relationships**: represent the relationships between the beam nodes, defect nodes, and optimization nodes.
## Knowledge Graph Schema
The Knowledge Graph schema is designed to be flexible and scalable, allowing for easy addition of new nodes and relationships. The schema consists of the following components:
* **Beam Properties**: properties of the wooden beams, such as length, width, and material.
* **Defect Characteristics**: characteristics of the defects, such as type, location, and severity.
* **Optimization Suggestions**: suggestions for optimizing the wooden beams, including repairs, replacements, and maintenance.
## Knowledge Graph Querying
The Knowledge Graph is designed to be queryable, allowing for easy retrieval of information about the wooden beams and their defects. The Knowledge Graph supports the following query types:
* **Beam Queries**: retrieve information about a specific beam, including its properties and defects.
* **Defect Queries**: retrieve information about a specific defect, including its type, location, and severity.
* **Optimization Queries**: retrieve optimization suggestions for a specific beam or defect.
